New to this exercise? Quick form checklist

Squat: Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Lower yourself until thighs are at least parallel to the floor. Keep chest up, weight on whole foot. Stand back up by pushing the floor away.

Deadlift: Stand with feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot. Hinge at hips, grip bar outside your knees, brace core, then stand up by pushing floor away. Keep the bar close to your body.

Bench Press: Lie on bench, feet flat on floor. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulders. Lower to chest, pause briefly, then press up until arms are straight.

Tip: Record your full set from the side view for best results. Front-angle recordings enable additional knee tracking analysis.

What we can and cannot detect

What we can detect

  • Squat depth (knee angle relative to hip)
  • Knee tracking / valgus (frontal plane projection angle)
  • Trunk angle and forward lean
  • Left/right symmetry (hip and knee angles)
  • Descent and ascent tempo
  • Lockout completeness (hip and knee extension)
  • Butt wink (posterior pelvic tilt at bottom)
  • Good morning squat pattern
  • Heel rise indicators
  • Sticking points and velocity changes
  • Rep-to-rep fatigue patterns
  • Deadlift: back rounding, hip shooting, hitching
  • Bench press: pause at chest, press evenness, lockout

What we cannot reliably detect

  • Foot pressure distribution (no force plate data)
  • Grip width or hand placement
  • Breathing and bracing patterns
  • Bar position on back (high bar vs low bar must be specified)
  • Internal joint forces or injury risk
  • Muscle activation patterns (would need EMG)
  • Spinal loading or disc compression
  • Exact bar path without markers
  • Weight on the bar (must be entered manually)

Important limitations

  • Camera angle significantly affects accuracy — side view is best for depth/trunk
  • Loose clothing can obscure joint positions
  • MediaPipe pose estimation has ~2-5° angle error
  • This tool provides form feedback, not medical advice
  • Always consult a qualified coach or physiotherapist for injury concerns
